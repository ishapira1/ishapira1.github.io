This website uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source, and interaction timing. I use these measurements to maintain and improve the website.

This site does not provide user accounts, accept comments, display advertising, or sell visitors' personal information. You can limit analytics cookies through your browser settings or privacy tools. For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
